# NOCD

> NOCD is a virtual mental-health provider specializing in the treatment of
> obsessive-compulsive disorder. It matches members with licensed therapists trained in
> Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), delivers live video sessions through its web and
> mobile applications, and provides between-session messaging, a peer community, and in-app
> therapeutic tools. NOCD publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable
> specification; its application backend (api.treatmyocd.com) is private to its own clients.
